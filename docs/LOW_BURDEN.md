## First principle (copy pointer)

The user-facing conclusion must let an impatient reader know **what to do**, **under what conditions**, and **what would flip it** — without a second “say it in human” pass.

Lowest burden ≠ shortest. Hiding a flip-condition raises burden. Dumping labor raises burden.
