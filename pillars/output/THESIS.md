# Pillar 4 · Structured output

## L0

Output exists to **solve the user’s problem**, not to display research labor.  
Lead with first-principle answer and main contradiction; structure is a servant.

---

## Default card shape

1. **Problem restatement** (end purpose in one sentence)  
2. **Main contradiction** (what this answer optimizes)  
3. **Working premises** (work-true claims + corroboration summary)  
4. **Answer / plan** (the knife — itinerary, decision, map, recommendation *with conditions*)  
5. **Corroboration table** (pointer or short)  
6. **Residuals / UNKNOWN** (loud)  
7. **What would change the answer** (falsifiers)  
8. **Sources** (short)

Confidence: state **once** per main conclusion (S/A/B/C or equivalent) — not badge spam on every line.

---

## Templates

Domain templates (travel, vendor pick, incident) may add sections — they must **not** bury the main knife under equal-weight facets.

Knowledge-store shape (L0–L3) is for **durable facts** (`floor-corpus`); the **user-facing deliverable** follows this pillar.

---

## break_condition

Reader remembers “lots of links” but not the decision; or confidence theater with no actionability.
